Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Mail Monitor
Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Purpose

OpenClaw Mail Monitor is a privately operated personal-use tool that monitors an authorized Gmail inbox, creates AI-assisted email summaries, and delivers notifications to the authorized account owner through Telegram. It is not a public email service.

2. Google user data accessed

With permission from the Google account owner using Google OAuth 2.0, the application accesses Gmail information needed to identify new messages and prepare summaries. This may include message identifiers, sender addresses, subject lines, timestamps, and message body content. The application does not request the account owner's Google password.

3. How data is used

Gmail data is used to check for new messages, generate requested summaries, deliver notifications, and maintain processing state to reduce duplicate notifications. The application does not sell Gmail data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. External service providers

OpenAI API: Relevant email data, including message content, may be transmitted to OpenAI for summarization. OpenAI processes this information according to its API policies. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the API customer explicitly opts in. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Telegram: Summaries, which may include information from the underlying messages, are sent through the Telegram Bot API to the authorized user's chat. Telegram processes those notifications according to its policies. See Telegram's Privacy Policy.

Gmail data is not intentionally transferred to advertisers or data brokers and is not used by the application to train generalized AI models.

5. Storage and retention

The privately managed server retains processing state needed to track checks and reduce duplicate notifications. Operational logs and temporary processing information may also be stored. Retention of locally controlled records depends on operational settings, and a fixed automatic-deletion period is not promised. The account owner may request review or deletion of locally retained information using the support address below.

Previously delivered summaries and data processed by external services may be retained under those providers' own policies. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete messages already delivered to Telegram.

6. Security

Google OAuth credentials and service secrets are managed within the private application environment and are not published through this informational website. Access is intended to be restricted to authorized operators. No technical security measure guarantees absolute protection.

7. Revocation and deletion requests

The Google account owner may revoke the application's access at any time from Google Account permissions. To request review or deletion of locally controlled monitoring data, contact the support address below. Requests will be evaluated and records under the operator's control deleted where applicable. Telegram notifications may require separate removal.

8. Google API Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements, and to remain limited to the email monitoring and summarization features requested by the authorized user.

9. Contact

For support and privacy inquiries, email openclaw-mail-monitor-support@googlegroups.com.

10. Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page if the application's practices or service providers change.
